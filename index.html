AI Case Studies

How artificial intelligence has transformed every stage of our production pipeline — from masterplan to marketing.

Vision before a single building exists

When a client arrives with a masterplan, all that exists is a boundary, a brief, and an ambition. We combine 25 years of project experience with AI to create city-scale precinct visions — imagery and cinematic video that go far beyond a site plan with coloured zones.

Winning hearts before the first sod is turned

The public reveal is the most delicate moment in a development's lifecycle. AI lets us produce media release imagery and video with the photorealistic quality and atmospheric conviction it takes to move public sentiment — at a fraction of the time it used to require.

Solving photomontage problems that used to have no good answer

Trees obscuring key sightlines. Buildings still standing that are scheduled for demolition. AI now lets us reconstruct exactly what a site will reveal once it's cleared — with the realism that would previously have required days of skilled manual work to approximate.

When the weather won't cooperate and the drone can't fly

Site photography is the foundation of accurate CGI, but weather and airspace regulations rarely cooperate with project timelines. AI now reconstructs unusable sections of base photography and shifts the time of day from midday into golden hour — work that once took a day now takes minutes.

Why the tool is never the differentiator

Every serious practitioner has access to the same headline AI tools. What separates results that sell from results that merely look impressive is decades of craft, the knowledge to direct and constrain those tools, and the creative eye no prompt can replicate.

From the most complained-about element to the most compelling

The people in CGI used to be its weakest ingredient — wrong demographic, wrong clothing, distracting from the architecture. Now we generate custom talent matched precisely to your buyer demographic, lit and staged so the people don't apologise for the image, they sell it.
